SLVF Sovereign Governance & Anti-Gaming Protocols

Mathematical and legal constraints preventing systemic metric exploitation

Audited Consensus
1. Elimination of Seat-Time & Throughput

Linear speed ($\Delta \text{Modules}/\Delta t$) is disqualified from impact rewards. Payouts require independently assayed retention ($\mathcal{R}(t)$) at 30-day delays.

2. Anti-AI Dependence Penalty

If a learner demonstrates high performance under AI scaffolding but fails the unassisted Meso-Crucible ($M_D^{\text{unassisted}} < 0.65$), the Sovraegent impact pool receives a zero-multiplier.

3. Privacy-Integrity Paradox Solution

Temenos raw data is never exposed. Telemetry passes solely through the ZKP Refractor, ensuring Guilds verify mathematical purity without commercial profiling.

4. Non-Negotiable Sovereignty Bounds

Interventions that accelerate factual recall while diminishing user self-regulation, privacy, or agency ($\Omega_{\text{intent}} < 0.6$) are algorithmically demoted from reference classes.
