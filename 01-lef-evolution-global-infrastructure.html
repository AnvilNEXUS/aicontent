Analytical Note on Verified vs. Target Figures

Distinguish operational traction (such as ScoutPass surpassing 200,000 issued verifiable credentials in 2024 and U.S. state LER pilots) from multi-year national targets (such as Nigeria's 22 million youth plan backed by $500 million in milestone-contingent World Bank expansion financing, or the 1 billion learner 2030 passport coalition). Independent third-party impact evaluations of these rollouts remain limited.
