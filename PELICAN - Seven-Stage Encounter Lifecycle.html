The CODE+V Socratic Heuristic Matrix

          

          

            Correcting transcript terminology discrepancies: Rather than an arbitrary score grid, CODE+V operationalizes: 
            Curate, Organize, Distill, Express, Verify, Validate.
            In PELICAN, these guide qualitative human inquiry rather than forcing divergent disciplines into a rigid robotic checklist.
          

        


        
        

          
          
          

            

              C • CURATE
              Inputs & Lineage
            

            
Sources, People, Influences & Lineage

            

              Examines what raw materials, domain priors, human collaborators, and AI scaffolding contributed to the work.
            

            

              Key Socratic Probe:
              
"Which foundations are independently generated versus derived, and are external contributions and model aids cleanly attributed?"

            

          


          
          

            

              O • ORGANIZE
              Structural Topology
            

            
Structure, Path & Information Architecture

            

              Examines how the learner architected the inquiry, structured modules, and sequenced evidence within the SYNCTESS.
            

            

              Key Socratic Probe:
              
"Does the internal architecture support logical navigation, or does it collapse when dependencies are tested out of order?"

            

          


          
          

            

              D • DISTILL
              Cognitive Essence
            

            
Core Insight & Conceptual Clarity

            

              Tests whether the learner has extracted core principles or merely produced verbose, decorative padding.
            

            

              Key Socratic Probe:
              
"Can the learner articulate the essential thesis in one minute, independent of decorative artifact polish?"

            

          


          
          

            

              E • EXPRESS
              Demonstration Fidelity
            

            
Artifact Fidelity, Performance & Medium

            

              Evaluates how effectively the executed medium (musical performance, software engine, doctoral thesis) manifests understanding.
            

            

              Key Socratic Probe:
              
"Does the tangible demonstration meet professional guild standards for clarity, aesthetic rigor, and ergonomic execution?"

            

          


          
          

            

              V • VERIFY
              Empirical Audit
            

            
Checks, Observations, Tests & Reproducibility

            

              The factual reality check: running unit tests, auditing citations, inspecting laboratory controls, and checking execution nonces.
            

            

              Key Socratic Probe:
              
"Can the claims be independently rerun or verified without the original author holding the controls?"

            

          


          
          

            

              V • VALIDATE
              Guild Authority
            

            
Mastery Justification against Domain Criteria

            

              Determines whether the verified evidence warrants sovereign credentialing, and whether the reviewing SOVRAEGE is authorized to attest.
            

            

              Key Socratic Probe:
              
"Does this achievement warrant recommendation to the Guild for formal SYNTHCRED synthesis?"
