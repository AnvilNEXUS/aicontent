SYNCZINE LIVING LABORATORY — COMPREHENSIVE CATALOG & ATLAS

THE CENTRAL IDEA A SYNCZINE is a digital composable decentralized learning-path container capable of turning a page into a book, an instrument, an experiment, a studio, a collaboration room or a place to demonstrate mastery. Canvas describes the learning environment; SYNCZ-SRLO describes the learning activity; SYNCMODZ and SAEGEMODZ describe contextual learner and guide tools; SYNCTESS relates capability, pedagogy and evidence; SYNTHCRED records authorized verified mastery.

This atlas is a curated proposed product taxonomy, not an inventory of already-built software. It synthesizes six supplied working papers and extends their concepts. Source-derived foundations and recommended new catalog entries are distinguished below.

1. Why five catalogs, not one

Layer Question it answers Example What it is NOT
Canvas Where am I learning? Voice laboratory A fixed technology or merely a visual theme
SYNCZ-SRLO What am I doing to learn? Construct and speak an appropriate request The whole lesson or learning path
SYNCMODZ Which learner tool helps me act? Recording console, phrase bank, notebook The learning objective itself
SAEGEMODZ Which guide tool helps me mentor, assess or verify? Rubric assessor and feedback composer Permission to observe everyone automatically
Composition How are these combined into an experience? Read → construct → role-play → reflect → demonstrate A new siloed application

A SYNCZINE organizes the designed pathway and its prerequisites. SOVRAELEX presents the learner's experience, and SOVRAEGIX presents authorized guide experiences. SYNCTESS should mean the mastery/evidence specification plus corresponding learner-specific evidence instances; an alternative infrastructure meaning appears in one source paper and needs an explicit renaming decision. SOVRAEZINE is the learner-controlled retained body of work and publication; SOVRAEPASS supplies identity, permissions and credential access. A SYNTHCRED is the separately authorized credential.

Keep the boundaries clear A Canvas is a presentation and working environment, an SRLO is an experience with pedagogical behavior, and a MODZ is a reusable instrument. An engine (Godot, audio, mapping, diagrams, etc.) is a possible implementation capability, not a type of learning objective. The 24 engines in the source Components paper should not turn into 24 mandatory app dependencies.

2. Six experience modes

Mode Learner's question UX behavior Meaningful outcomes
READ What should I understand? Accessible texts, translations, media, examples, context Notes, citations, conceptual understanding
EXPLORE What can I notice or discover? Zoom, inspect, map, compare, navigate, reveal Observations, annotations, discoveries
EXPERIMENT What happens if I change this? Control variables, predict, repeat, compare Hypotheses, trials, results, explanations
COLLABORATE What can we solve or create together? Shared objects, roles, peer dialogue, local group session Group artifact plus individual contribution traces
REFLECT What have I learned and how do I know? Review attempts, critique, journal, compare outcomes Revisions, reflections, evidence selection
DEMONSTRATE Can I perform the capability independently? Authentic challenge with defined rubric and support policy Submitted performance, assessor judgment, verification

A single Canvas archetype can support multiple modes. The mode changes the intent and allowed interaction, not necessarily the entire visual layout. Do not confuse mode, archetype, theme, subject matter or screen size.

CANVAS interpretation rules

  1. One Canvas may contain multiple SRLO, just as one workbench may hold several experiments.
  2. An SRLO may appear in multiple Canvas archetypes. Sentence Forge can be solo, collaborative or embedded in a role-play.
  3. A Canvas should remain intelligible if a specialist visualization cannot run. Offer reading, simplified data, static images or other accessible alternatives.
  4. The six modes may be exposed as tabs, progressive steps or context-dependent actions; not every page must show all six controls.
  5. Visual skins are not new Canvas archetypes. Clean scientific and dark brass themes can present the same learning behavior.

Role and trust principles for MODZ

  • Learner and guide tools should have symmetrical vocabulary where useful (Lab Notebook ↔ Evidence Inspector), but not symmetrical access rights.
  • AI assistance is advisory unless there is an independently approved, explicit assessment policy. Human-authorized validation remains a separate act.
  • Widgets are contextual, not permanently open, and their visibility is independent of whether underlying evidence exists.
  • A tool may suggest a candidate evidence item, but it cannot claim mastery by itself.
  • Permissioning must remain visible, scoped and auditable; previously exported copies cannot always be technically revoked.

Why this library can scale

A Sentence Builder is an archetype, not one lesson. An author can configure it for polite requests, past-tense narratives, safety instructions or workplace communication without rebuilding the interaction. SRLO variations should change content, language, feedback, difficulty, collaborative arrangements and evidence expectations through published authoring settings, without multiplying the base library.

7. Reusable experience compositions

A composition is a tested recipe linking Canvas archetypes, SRLO, MODZ and mastery outcomes. Authors should select a pattern and adapt it, rather than invent the pedagogical sequence from scratch.

Recipe 01 — ZERO TO VOICE: SENTENCE FORGE

  • Audience / goal: Adult EFL learner in Vietnam; make an appropriate service request.
  • CANVAS: C01 Living Book → C16 Sentence Forge → C22 Role-Play Stage → C27 Performance Stage.
  • SRLO: S07 Sentence Builder → S11 Rule Transformer → S23 Dialogue Turn-Taking → S59 Mastery Demonstration.
  • SYNCMODZ: L16 Phrase Bank, L17 Bilingual Lens, L13 Recorder, L19 Notebook, L31 Evidence Collector.
  • SAEGEMODZ: G09 Socratic Prompt, G11 Feedback, G13 Rubric Assessor.
  • SYNCTESS: Performance should show appropriate request, intelligible response and responsive clarification across more than one context.
  • Learner keeps: Selected sentence revisions, recordings/observations, feedback and a SOVRAEZINE reflection.

Recipe 02 — GAEA CORE: SEISMIC PRESSURE LAB

  • Audience / goal: Understand causal relationships in a simplified model of tectonic stress.
  • CANVAS: C04 Concept Observatory → C11 Variable Workbench → C13 Data Lab → C28 Evidence Desk.
  • SRLO: S13 Predict–Test–Explain → S14 Parameter Sweep → S31 Tectonic Stress Lab → S42 Model Critique.
  • SYNCMODZ: L07 Controller, L08 Experiment Planner, L09 Measurement Console, L10 Compare & Replay, L19 Notebook.
  • SAEGEMODZ: G05 Observer (permissioned), G09 Socratic Prompt, G13 Rubric Assessor.
  • SYNCTESS: Explain predictions, measurements, sources of uncertainty and limits of model-based conclusions.
  • Learner keeps: Hypotheses, trial settings, results, annotated graphs and assessor feedback.

Recipe 03 — COOPERATIVE SERVICE COUNTER

  • Audience / goal: Handle a café order or workplace information exchange using respectful conversation.
  • CANVAS: C22 Role-Play Stage → C21 Shared Table → C29 Feedback & Revision Chamber.
  • SRLO: S25 Branching Conversation, S26 Service Encounter, S51 Peer Teaching, S54 Peer Review.
  • SYNCMODZ: L27 Role Selector, L30 Mentor Request, L29 Contribution Ledger, L22 Reflection Prompter.
  • SAEGEMODZ: G02 Group Facilitator, G12 Demonstration Annotator.
  • Mastery condition: Group success is insufficient; each participant must independently demonstrate relevant individual capability.

Recipe 04 — DATA DETECTIVE

  • Audience / goal: Challenge a claim with appropriate local data.
  • CANVAS: C09 Evidence Board → C13 Data Lab → C20 Publication Studio.
  • SRLO: S17 Data Hypothesis Test, S37 Chart Detective, S38 Dataset Query, S44 Explainer Builder.
  • SYNCMODZ: L21 Source Basket, L09 Measurement Console, L19 Notebook, L35 SOVRAEZINE Curator.
  • SAEGEMODZ: G07 Misconception Analyzer, G11 Feedback Composer, G14 Provenance Checker.
  • Learner keeps: Data citations, reproducible analysis, limitations and a published explanation.

Recipe 05 — DESIGN & TEST ATELIER

  • Audience / goal: Create and validate a simple engineered prototype.
  • CANVAS: C19 Maker Workshop → C11 Workbench → C23 Peer Critique Gallery → C27 Performance Stage.
  • SRLO: S36 Design & Tolerance Test, S18 Fault Diagnosis, S47 Prototype Challenge, S54 Structured Peer Review.
  • SYNCMODZ: L11 Object Palette, L10 Compare & Replay, L19 Notebook, L31 Evidence Collector.
  • SAEGEMODZ: G03 Activity Configuration, G19 Quality Reviewer, G13 Rubric Assessor.
  • Learner keeps: Designs, revisions, test evidence and a defense of trade-offs.

Recipe 06 — SOVEREIGN PUBLICATION & CREDENTIAL SHARING

  • Audience / goal: Demonstrate a previously learned skill to a particular audience without handing over an entire private learning record.
  • CANVAS: C28 Evidence Desk → C20 SOVRAEZINE Publication Studio → C30 Credential Gallery.
  • SRLO: S48 Learner Zine Edition, S59 Authentic Mastery Demonstration, S60 Evidence Defense.

9. Evidence, sovereignty and the learner-owned SOVRAEZINE

CODE+V as a visible learning journey

Stage What the learner does in the UX Typical components
Curate Collect trusted sources and relevant examples C09, L21, S02
Organize Assemble a route or conceptual structure C05, L20, S45
Distill Explain patterns and reduce noise C04, L19, S44
Express Create an authentic artifact or perform C16/C19/C22, S47/S59
Verify Receive feedback and an assessment tied to criteria C29, G11/G13, S60
Validate Obtain a decision or claim from an authorized party C28/C30, G16/G24

Evidence maturity ladder

Unrecorded exploration → personal practice record → learner-selected candidate evidence → submitted evidence packet → reviewed and verified mastery → separately issued SYNTHCRED.

These are distinct statuses. An activity can generate valuable observations without constituting qualifying proof. Automated success, total time or entertainment scores should not silently become a mastery claim.

Where things belong

Belongs to Responsibility
SYNCZINE Published curriculum, path dependencies, experience composition and versions
SYNCZ-SRLO Activity behavior, inputs, feedback and candidate artifacts
SYNCTESS specification Capability, rubric, evidence requirement, authorized verification policy
Learner-specific evidence instance Actual candidate and submitted work with version context
SOVRAEZINE Learner-controlled retained references, reflections, creations, work, feedback and directed publications
SLR / SOVRAEPASS Learning record, identity, permissions and access to credentials
SYNTHCRED Authorized, verifiable claim of mastery, not a substitute for all supporting work

Guardrails

  1. No hidden capture; learner can see what is recorded.
  2. No group result used as automatic individual mastery proof.
  3. No AI silently changing a rubric or issuing credentials without authorized policy.
  4. Reading and essential instruction remain usable without rich interactive engines.
  5. Solo learning is local-first; group network collaboration is additive.
  6. Accessibility, localization and reduced-motion alternatives are first-class.
  7. Guide access is purpose-limited and permissioned; revocation cannot undo copies already exported.
  8. Keep a meaningful version link between published activity, assessment rules and evidence.
  9. Protect assessment integrity without imposing surveillance on ordinary practice.
  10. Richer simulations must show better learning outcomes than simpler alternatives before being preferred.

10. Catalog metadata and quality standard

Every catalog entry should be inspectable in SYNCFORGE and reviewed in SYNCFOUNDRY through an author-friendly card. This is a plain-language metadata specification, not an implementation schema:

Field Why it matters
Stable ID and public title Distinguishes archetypes from local adaptations
Catalog family and purpose Makes browsing and discovery intuitive
What learner sees and does Explains the observable experience
Intended learner and context Supports appropriate age, level, culture, language and device
Learning objective and associated SYNCTESS Aligns experience to demonstrated capability
Recommended Canvas and compatible experience modes Prevents poorly fitting combinations
Required and optional MODZ Keeps the interface uncluttered
Inputs, outputs and saved artifacts Makes evidence and data ownership clear
Collaboration and assistive options Describes accessible individual or group use
Accessibility, offline and device profile Avoids designing exclusively for expensive hardware
Privacy, permissions and recordings policy Enables meaningful user consent
Source, author, license, version and adaptation rights Protects provenance and legitimate reuse
Complexity and performance footprint Helps choose the lightest viable technology
Test results and maintenance owner Prevents abandoned plug-ins or misleading quality claims

Three levels of reuse

  • Archetype: the general learning pattern, for example S07 Sentence Builder.
  • Template: a reusable configured experience, for example polite requests in cafés.
  • Instance: a specific assigned session and the learner's resulting work and evidence.

Quality gate for publishing an entry

An entry should be publishable only after a reviewer can answer: What capability does it develop? Why is this interaction preferable to simpler alternatives? What can the learner change? What results are meaningful? What can be retained or submitted? Is it accessible and local-first where promised? Who owns the result? What version and rights are in force?

Recommended decisions to ratify

  • Name: standardize SAEGEMODZ in specifications, and accept SAGEMODZ as a transitional alias if useful.
  • SYNCTESS: reserve the term for the tangible mastery/evidence concept; rename its conflicting usage as an infrastructure/data-availability layer.
  • CANVAS vs mode: formally approve archetype, mode, layout/theme and implementation capability as separate descriptors.
  • SRLO scope: agree that the 60 entries are archetype definitions, not 60 independent engines.
  • SOVRAEZINE / SLR / wallet boundary: decide where originals, references, evidence, permissions and credential pointers are retained.
  • Recording policies: distinguish informal practice, explicit submission and formal evaluation.
  • Optional ownership economics: do not make blockchain royalty infrastructure a prerequisite for ordinary learning or publishing.
  • Evidence standard: define exactly what merits human review, and preserve the assessment rule version.
  • Catalog governance: require an owner, review state, maintenance plan and deprecation path for every reusable component.

Closing principle Build an atlas of learning behaviors before building an arsenal of software engines. A small set of excellent, composable experiences that reliably turn learning into defensible evidence will create a stronger foundation than hundreds of disconnected widgets.
